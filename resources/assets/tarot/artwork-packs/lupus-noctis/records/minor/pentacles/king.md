Its emotional center is confidence grounded in the real cost of land, labor, time, and reserves rather than displayed wealth. Upright emphasizes practical intelligence, business ability, reliability, success, and materially grounded authority; the materially relevant reversed meaning is corruption, greed, stagnation, control, vice, or wealth used to bind people.
