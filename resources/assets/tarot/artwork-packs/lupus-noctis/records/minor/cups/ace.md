## Researched Meaning Summary

Ace of Cups is an emotional beginning: joy, receptivity, compassion, renewed trust, love, healing, and willingness to connect. Its constructive meaning is the first opening through which tenderness can grow; its risks include idealization, emotional flooding, dependency, premature intimacy, and confusing intensity with trust. Materially relevant reversed meaning includes blocked feeling, exhaustion, mistrust, false intimacy, and an opening that cannot yet be accepted.
