## Researched Meaning Summary

King of Cups represents emotional command, compassion under pressure, diplomacy, maturity, and remaining receptive without being overwhelmed. Constructively it supports emotional stability, calm leadership, empathy, responsibility, containment, listening, and measured response. Its risks are manipulation, detachment disguised as calm, suppressing necessary anger, paternalism, reassurance used as control, and composure that refuses vulnerability.

One stationary gimballed cup of level water quietly opposes the rolling deck.
