## Researched Meaning Summary

The Five of Cups concerns loss and regret that narrow attention until a person sees only what was destroyed and cannot yet notice what remains. Its emotional center is quiet grief after the event, when the world continues but the mourner is not ready to move with it. Constructively it supports acknowledging grief and regret, accepting what cannot be restored, eventually noticing surviving relationships or resources, and beginning reconciliation with loss. Its tensions are fixation on failure, self-blame, bitterness, withdrawal, and grief treated as permanent identity. Upright emphasizes loss, regret, disappointment, sorrow, and attention fixed on what was spilled; the materially relevant reversed meaning is the first return toward connection and what survives.
