## Researched Meaning Summary

Three of Swords represents heartbreak, grief, painful truth, divided loyalty, and an irreversible wound to relationship. Constructively it acknowledges loss, accepts painful truth, mourns without denial, ends destructive division, and recognizes the cost of loyalty split between incompatible sides. Its risks are despair, fixation on betrayal, self-punishment, cruelty, grief becoming identity, and using pain to justify revenge.
