## Researched Meaning Summary

Strength is conscious command of a powerful impulse: courage, patience, and trust accomplishing what brute suppression cannot. Its emotional center is calm beside danger, preserving another being's dignity while creating conditions for self-command to return. Constructively it supports courage, compassion, endurance, restraint, patience, integration of instinct, and power without humiliation. Its tensions are repression mistaken for control, domination hidden beneath care, pride in fearlessness, exhaustion, unnecessary risk, and compassion without boundaries. Upright emphasizes power, energy, magnanimity, confidence, and self-command; the materially relevant reversed meaning is lost self-command, misuse of force, exhaustion, domination, or instinct no longer held consciously.
