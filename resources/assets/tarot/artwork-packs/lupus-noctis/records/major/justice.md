## Researched Meaning Summary

Justice concerns impartial comparison of facts, consequences, and obligations rather than a display of power. Its emotional center is the cold clarity of deciding by evidence even when neither side receives everything it wants. Constructively it supports equity, truth, law, accountability, proportional consequence, balanced judgement, intellectual honesty, restoration, and fair boundaries. Its tensions are compassionless legalism, hidden bias, selective evidence, punishment mistaken for justice, neutrality that protects power, and procedural cruelty. Upright emphasizes equity, rightness, law, judgement, executive decision, and restored proportion; the materially relevant reversed meaning is bias, false accusation, unequal law, manipulation, excessive severity, or refusal of consequence.
