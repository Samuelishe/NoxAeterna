## Researched Meaning Summary

Temperance is the active joining of opposites into a stable proportion: precise process control rather than weakness or refusal to act. Its emotional center is calm concentration that prevents heat, cold, fear, or desire from taking control. Constructively it supports moderation, synthesis, patience, healing, adaptation, integration, disciplined flow, and cooperation between unlike forces. Its tensions are false compromise, dilution of principle, endless adjustment, emotional suppression, exhaustion used to maintain balance, and fear of intensity. Upright emphasizes economy, accommodation, management, synthesis, and measured healing; the materially relevant reversed meaning is excess, poor combination, incompatible elements, or an unstable process whose proportions are no longer controlled.
